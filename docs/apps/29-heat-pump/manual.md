Heat Pump is a clock-synced sidechain ducking envelope: on each division the CV ducks, holds briefly, then recovers with a logarithmic release. Patch CV Out to a filter or VCA; MIDI CC mirrors when Out is enabled.

Main sets release speed. Shift + fader sets depth. Button + fader picks clock division. Short tap triggers one manual duck. Long press mutes without snapping to idle. Shift + long toggles invert.
