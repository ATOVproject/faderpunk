Clock-driven chord sequencer cycling through three tonal centers spaced by a configurable interval (default major 3rd). Density fader controls approach depth in 7 positions on a fixed 9-slot grid — three slots (ii, V, I) per center. The I of each center always plays; raising the fader inserts one approach chord at a time, first the V of centers 1–3, then the ii of centers 1–3, so the cycle grows 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 chords. Empty slots aren’t silent — the previous chord sustains through them, so the cycle stays nine slots long at every density and sparse settings give a long held tonic. Nine slots sit across 4/4, so the cycle returns to a downbeat every nine bars on binary divisions and every three on triplet ones. Chord qualities follow Coltrane convention: Maj7 on centers, Dom7 on dominants, m7 on subdominant approaches. Voicing param sets close triad, 7th, drop-2 or quartal stacking, and each chord is revoiced to move as little as possible from the one before it.

Button tap mutes, cutting anything still held; hold toggles reverse. Shift + tap resets the cycle to step 0, shift + hold cycles the interval through minor 3rd, major 3rd, 4th and tritone. Direction param adds pendulum and random modes on top. CV out tracks the root pitch of the current step and parks at 0 V while muted; CV in modulates density or triggers reset.

Shift + fader is Feel. At the bottom the sequence is straight and flat, exactly as before; further up the off-beats swing and the dynamics open out, accenting the tonic while the approach chords lighten. Feel steps aside when the device’s own swing is active so the two never stack. Hold the button and move the fader for Time: it picks the step division outright across all eleven values, longest first — 1/1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/4t, 1/8, 1/8t, 1/16, 1/16t, 1/32, 1/32t, 1/64t. The Division param only sets where the app starts; the fader overrides it live.

Motion decides how far the harmony is allowed to wander. Straight is the plain engine. Rubato lets the tonic lean, holding twice as long while the ii and V keep passing. Sheets breaks each chord into a fast run up and back down instead of a block. Free adds tritone substitutions on roughly every third dominant.

Top and bottom LEDs show three distinct hues for the three tonal centers, rotated around the app colour. The button reads out the harmony itself: its hue sways off the current center hue with the harmonic function — the tonic sits on the center, its V a little away, its ii further still — while its brightness rises with Density across the seven steps, following the CV In Density offset when that jack mode is active. It dips briefly on each chord trigger, fades white to none when reverse toggles, and flashes a colour per step when the interval cycles.
