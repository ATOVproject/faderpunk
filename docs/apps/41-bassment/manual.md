Genre MIDI bass on the global clock (9 shared genres with Grooves / Chord Vamp — tropes live in `genre_palette::GENRE_PROG_8`). Density reveals fill hits and freer scale degrees; Alt scrubs genre on the shared spectrum axis; Third is Feel / Groove (swing, microtiming, velocity contrast, ghost drag, gate character — depth capped by Groove max %). Notes sustain across empty 16ths until the next hit (legato when the pitch repeats).

Phrases are 8 bars in AA B A′ form: the rhythmic motif repeats, harmony walks the tropes, bars 5–6 answer (shifted, more approaches), bar 7 ornaments, bar 8 breathes (no ghosts/approaches). At higher Feel the line leaves “lock” mode: live Dice mix with the step-hash for approaches, ghosts, octave punches, and accent rake; pickups appear before downbeats, soft cores get dropped for air, ghosts can become dead notes, and approaches may be chromatic or scale-neighbor. Voice × genre soft-bias: pocket players stay sparse in Jungle/UKG; busy voices thin in Dub/Techno.

**Shift+Tap** plays a dedicated bass fill that walks toward the *next* bar’s chord (leading tone on the last hit) and resolves on the downbeat — Voice picks the contour (walk / arp / octave push / pedal); pocket Voices and Dub lean Pedal. At high Density the same gesture becomes a quiet pedal break (held root). **Shift+Hold** escalates into a register-lifted solo (+1 octave, +2 for Jaco/Claypool) with breath gaps and shorter gates; it re-rolls each bar while held and always resolves on the next downbeat. Button LED goes white for the gesture (dim = pedal break).

Long cycles bassist Voice personas forward in historical era order (Mingus → Jamerson → Bootsy → Jaco → Robbie → Flabba → Flea → Claypool; button flash = voice color). Previous Voice lives in the Configurator; Swing Dir rides the sign of Groove max %. Short mutes. CV Out is pitch (V/Oct); CV In modulates Density / Feel or triggers Reset. **Filter CC** (0 = off; 74 is typical cutoff) tracks the line the same way the velocities do: a genre brightness lerped along the shared spectrum (Dub and Dubstep deep, UK Garage and Jungle cutting), the Voice persona’s bite on top (Jamerson and Robbie stay warm, Flea and Claypool bright), and the hit’s own dynamics so accents open the filter. Fills lift, solos bloom, the pedal break ducks away. MIDI note-ons and filter CC fire outside the clock subscriber (`pending_*` isolate), same pattern as Grooves.

**Follow device tonic** (default on) makes the device Tonic a live transpose: hold the Scene button and move Fader 5, and Bassment moves with Contura, Chord Vamp, Venn and Arp de Lévy. It takes the pitch class from the device and keeps the octave of Root, so the line stays in its register; the new key lands on the next bar line. **Follow device scale** (default off) does the same for Scale — left off, the patch keeps its own, because Scale is part of the genre character.
