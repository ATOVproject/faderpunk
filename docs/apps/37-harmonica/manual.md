One root (last MIDI note or pitch CV) picks the chord root; the fader picks
chord type (Unison · Power · Min · Maj · Sus4 · Min7 · Dom7 · Maj7 · Min9 ·
Add9); up to four MIDI voices sound (MIDI→CV follows the highest). Alt sets
inversion (0–2) or open top. Not a polyphonic keyboard input.

Mute is a short tap — use Third (button+fader) for velocity so mute and vel
don’t collide. Alt+Short cycles harmony octave; Alt+Long walks voicing presets
(tight/dry → wide/slow). CV Gate: Sustain, fixed ms, or clock divisions.
Near-0V CV = rest; unpatched float stays silent until a stable pitch.
