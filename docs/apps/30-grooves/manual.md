Grooves is a one-channel multi-genre drum helper with up to seven MIDI voices clock-synced in 4/4. **Groove** picks the genre family; **Density** and **Feel** shape fills and character; **Drummer** personas (long press) change ornament style. Swing depth is capped by **Swing max %** (sign = direction). Jack modes include Any/Stacked CV out and CV in for Density, Feel, or Reset. Clock-driven scheduling stays in a dedicated processor; MIDI sends run in a separate task (`pending_*` isolation).
